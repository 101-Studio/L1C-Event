Event Strategy

Start with the decision, not the venue

By the L1C team · 10 Sep 2026 · 4 min read

← All articles

Most event briefs begin with a date and a venue. It works better to begin with a sentence: after this event, we want these people to have done this. A client signed, a partnership started, a team agreed on a plan.

Once that sentence exists, most other choices get easier. It tells you who needs to be in the room, how long they should stay, and whether you need a stage at all or simply a long table and twelve chairs.

It also gives you something to measure. If the goal was three follow-up meetings, count the follow-up meetings. That is a better report than attendance figures.
