Why in-person still matters in a world of video calls

By the L1C team · 01 Sep 2026 · 3 min read

← All articles

Video calls are very good at moving information. They are less good at the things that happen around the information: the quiet word before a session, the second coffee, the moment you realise the person next to you is dealing with the same problem.

Those moments are why people still travel to meet. A room full of peers gives a leader something a call cannot, which is a sense of how others are thinking about the same decisions and the chance to ask a follow-up question straight away.

That is also the argument for keeping events small, well hosted and worth the journey. If people are going to give up a day, the day should earn it.
